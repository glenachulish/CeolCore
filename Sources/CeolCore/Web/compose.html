How to…

Put notes on the stave

Choose a note length along the bottom, then tap the stave at the height you want: on a line or in a space. The note goes in at the ▾. Or tap a letter button: D E F G A B are the low octave, c d e f g a b the high one. Each bar fills from its empty space, and when it is full the ▾ moves on to the next bar by itself, so you never need to place bar lines.

Note lengths and dotted notes

The buttons are, in order: semiquaver, quaver, dotted quaver, crotchet, dotted crotchet, minim, dotted minim, semibreve. The length you choose stays chosen until you choose another. A reel is mostly quavers; a jig is quavers in threes, with dotted crotchets for the long notes.

Change or take out a note

Tap a note to select it (it turns gold). Then: a length button changes its length; ▲ ▼ move it up or down a step; a letter button or a tap on the stave replaces it; ⌫ takes it out and leaves its space empty. Without a selected note, ⌫ takes out the note before the ▾. ◀ ▶ move the ▾. ↩ undoes anything.

Sharps, flats and naturals

The key signature does the usual sharps for you: in D, every F and C is already sharp. For anything else, tap ♯, ♭ or ♮ just before the note; it applies to that one note (and, as in written music, to the same note later in the bar).

Rests, ties and triplets

Rest puts a rest of the chosen length at the ▾. Tie joins the next note to the one after it (play them as one long note). (3 makes the next three notes a triplet, three in the time of two: choose quavers, tap (3, then the three notes.

Start with a pickup

Many tunes start with a note or two before the first full bar. Tap Pickup, then the notes: they go before the first bar. Strictly, the last bar of the part then gives up the same amount of time; leave the end of that bar empty and Fonn fills it with a rest when you save.

Repeats, endings and more parts

Each part is already repeated: |: at the start, :| at the end. For different endings, put the ▾ in the part and tap 1st & 2nd endings: the part's last bar becomes the first-time bar, and a new empty bar is added for the second time. + Part adds another eight bars (C, D …). Parts you leave empty are left out when you save.

Title, type, key and metre

Fill them in at the top. Choosing a type before you write sets its metre (a jig is 6/8, a slip jig 9/8, a polka 2/4). Once there are notes, a new type changes only the name; to change the metre then, switch to ABC and edit the M: line.

Chords and ornaments

Easiest after saving: on the tune's ☰, Edit the chords lets you choose a chord for each bar, and Swing and ornaments adds rolls, cuts and other regional ornaments. In ABC, a chord is its name in quotes before the note, "G"B2, and a grace note goes in braces, {g}A.

Typing ABC instead

Tap ABC to see and type the tune as text; the music is redrawn as you type, and Stave takes you back. With a keyboard, Tab puts in a bar line. In a reel (L:1/8):

When the layout looks strange

Each line of ABC starts a new stave, so keep each part on one line (Fonn fits the bars to the screen). A blank line ends the tune, and anything after it is lost. A ! on its own forces a stave break. Text pasted from websites can carry invisible spaces. In ABC view, Fonn lists any of these it finds, and Tidy the layout fixes them without changing a note.

Hear it and keep it

▶ plays the tune as it will be kept. Save (Mac: Add to Library) takes out the empty bars and parts, tops up half-filled bars with rests, and adds the tune to your library, where it works like any other: sets, collections, versions, printing.
